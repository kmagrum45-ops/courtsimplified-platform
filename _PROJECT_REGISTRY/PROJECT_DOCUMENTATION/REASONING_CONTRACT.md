# CourtSimplified Reasoning Contract

This developer-only contract evaluates the existing canonical chain:

`Intake → CourtSimplifiedBrain → BrainMigrationLayer → MasterCaseSchema → CaseSystemAssembly → Specialized Intelligence Systems → Workflow → UI`

It is not a runtime case store, legal source, form catalogue, or authority database. It does not establish legal accuracy.

Every completed intake result must contain these fields, populated only from saved intake facts and existing specialized outputs:

1. `factualSnapshot` — court area, parties and role when entered, stage, requested outcome, amount when entered, and saved story.
2. `possibleIssuesToReview` — non-conclusive issue signals with fact-based explanations.
3. `evidenceRecorded` — only evidence actually entered by the person.
4. `evidenceToOrganizeOrConfirm` — proof gaps or evidence links supplied by the canonical intelligence output.
5. `contradictionsOrUncertainty` — conflicts, uncertain facts, or review-required gaps; never fabricated facts.
6. `proceduralStatus` — only recorded stage, filing, and service facts.
7. `nextConfirmationQuestion` — one focused question needed to continue safely.
8. `courtClarificationPoints` — neutral matters the court may need clarified.
9. `verifiedFormsAndProcedure` — present only when an existing exact official-source mapping supports it; otherwise review-required.
10. `reviewBoundary` — the product organizes facts and identifies items to review; it does not decide a legal outcome.
11. `nextWorkflowAction` — one existing workflow action, scoped to the selected case and court area.

Forbidden output includes outcome prediction, legal representation claims, invented facts or authority, unverified forms/procedure represented as confirmed, internal IDs, save status, AI configuration, fallback language, raw warnings, and error diagnostics.

The scenario registry uses synthetic/anonymized material only. Passing deterministic checks is software verification, not legal review or confirmation of a legal conclusion.
