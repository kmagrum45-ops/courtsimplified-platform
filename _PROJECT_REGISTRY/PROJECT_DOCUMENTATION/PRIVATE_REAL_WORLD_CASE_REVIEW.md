# Private real-world case review

Use this manual checklist outside the repository when reviewing a private case.

- Confirm the role is correct.
- Confirm the current stage is correct.
- Confirm recorded filed and service facts are preserved.
- Confirm no facts were invented.
- Confirm there is one focused next question.
- Confirm evidence and proof guidance is useful.
- Confirm no legal outcome is predicted.
- Confirm no internal or system wording is visible.
