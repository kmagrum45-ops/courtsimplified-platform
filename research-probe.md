  - Rules of the Small Claims Court, r. 13.03 (1)-(3): "13.03 (1) The purposes of a settlement conference are, (a) to resolve or narrow the issues in the action; (b) to expedite the disposition of the action; (c) to encourage settlement of the action; (d) to assist the parties in effective preparation for trial; and (e) to provide full disclosure between the parties of the relevant facts and evidence."
  - Rules of the Small Claims Court, r. 13.04: "13.04 The court may make recommendations to the parties on any matter relating to the conduct of the action, in order to fulfil the purposes of a settlement conference, including recommendations as to, (a) the clarification and simplification of issues in the action; (b) the elimination of claims or defences that appear to be unsupported; and (c) the admission of facts or documents without further proof."
  - Rules of the Small Claims Court, r. 13.05 (1)-(3): "13.05 (1) A judge conducting a settlement conference may make any order relating to the conduct of the action that the court could make."
- **ANSWERED** — Who must attend the settlement conference, and what requirements apply to representatives and authority to settle?
  - Rules of the Small Claims Court, r. 13.02 (1)-(5): "13.02 (1) A party and the party's representative, if any, shall participate in the settlement conference, unless the court orders otherwise."
  - Guide to Procedures in Small Claims Court: Getting ready for court: "The parties must attend, but the witnesses do not attend."
  - Rules of the Small Claims Court, r. 2 (6)-(9): "1.08 For greater certainty, nothing in these rules permits or authorizes the court to permit a person to act as a representative if that person is not authorized to do so under the Law Society Act."
- **ANSWERED** — How should a party confirm whether attendance is in person or remote, and request an adjournment or accommodation if unable to attend as directed?
