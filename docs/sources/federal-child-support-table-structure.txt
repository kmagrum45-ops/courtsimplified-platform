*** THE AMOUNTS ARE DELIBERATELY NOT HERE ***

This file records the SHAPE of the table and nothing else. No income bands, no
basic amounts, no percentages. A vendored copy of the numbers would become a
number the site could look up, and the whole child support design exists
because the table cannot be looked up: it takes Guidelines income, which
O. Reg. 391/97 s. 16 defines as T1 "Total income" adjusted by Schedule III, and
which ss. 17 to 20 let a court determine differently again.

Read the table at the URL above.

*** WHICH TABLE ***

There is one table and it is this one, on both paths. O. Reg. 303/24 revoked
Schedule I of O. Reg. 391/97 and rewrote its s. 2 (1) to point here. Which
PROVINCE'S table applies turns on where the parent or spouse against whom the
order is sought ordinarily resides — not where the case is filed, not where the
child lives. See ontario-child-support-guidelines.txt for the quoted definition.

*** THE STRUCTURE, VERBATIM ***

Each table is headed by the province and the number of children, and carries
two tiers of column headings:

  Province : Ontario
  No. of Children : One

  | Income ($)  | Monthly Award ($)                       |
  | From | To   | Basic Amount | Plus (%) | Of Income Over |

A separate table follows for each number of children — "No. of Children : Two"
and so on — with the same column structure.

The award for a row is NOT the Basic Amount alone. It is the Basic Amount plus
the Plus (%) applied to the income above the "Of Income Over" figure, which is
the floor of that row's band. That is what makes the table a formula rather
than a cell, and it is why "find the row, read the number" produces a wrong
answer for every income that is not exactly on a band floor.
