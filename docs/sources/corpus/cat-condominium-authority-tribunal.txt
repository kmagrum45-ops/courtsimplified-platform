 Dispute Resolution

 Condo communities can resolve their disputes conveniently, quickly and affordably through the Condominium Authority Tribunal, Canada’s first fully online tribunal. The CAT uses a dispute resolution system called CAT-ODR .

				 
 
 
 
 Regulation 179/17 defines the scope of disputes that can be resolved by the Tribunal.
