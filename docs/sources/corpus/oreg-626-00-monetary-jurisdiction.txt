                            Courts of Justice Act

                          ONTARIO REGULATION 626/00

              SMALL CLAIMS COURT JURISDICTION AND APPEAL LIMIT

CONSOLIDATION PERIOD:  FROM OCTOBER 1, 2025 TO THE E-LAWS CURRENCY DATE.

Last amendment: 42/25.

Legislative History: 439/08, 244/10, 317/11, 343/19, 42/25.

This is the English version of a bilingual regulation.

Jurisdiction
      1.  (1)  The maximum amount of a claim in the Small Claims Court  is
$50,000.  O. Reg. 626/00, s. 1 (1); O. Reg. 439/08, s. 1 (1);  O.  Reg.
343/19, s. 1; O. Reg. 42/25, s. 1.
      (2)  The maximum amount of a claim over  which  a  deputy  judge  may
preside is $50,000.  O. Reg. 626/00, s. 1 (2); O. Reg. 439/08, s. 1 (2); O.
Reg. 343/19, s. 1; O. Reg. 42/25, s. 1.

Appeal limit
      2.  (1)  For the purposes of clause 31 (a) of the Act, the prescribed
amount is $5,000.  O. Reg. 317/11, s. 1; O. Reg. 343/19,  s.  2;  O.  Reg.
42/25, s. 2.
      (2)  For the purposes of clause 31 (b) of  the  Act,  the  prescribed
amount is $5,000.  O. Reg. 317/11, s. 1 O. Reg.  343/19,  s.  2;  O.  Reg.
42/25, s. 2.
      3.  Omitted (provides for coming into force  of  provisions  of  this
Regulation).  O. Reg. 626/00, s. 3.
