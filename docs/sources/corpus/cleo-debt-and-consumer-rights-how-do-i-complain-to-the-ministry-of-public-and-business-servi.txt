 How do I complain to the Ministry of Public and Business Service Delivery?

 Next steps

 1. Learn what rules the companies have to follow

 2. Write a complaint letter

 3. File a complaint with the Ministry

 4. Get a response from the Ministry

 5. Go to Small Claims Court

 The Ontario Consumer Protection Act has rules and laws to protect consumers.
 You can complain to the Ministry of Public and Business Service Delivery if any of the following kinds of businesses have not followed these rules:

 door-to-door seller
 consumer reporting agency
 credit repair company
 debt settlement company
 payday loan company
 collection agency

 You can complain about a good or service that you bought at a store, online , by mail , or by phone .
 The Ministry will look at your complaint and decide what to do based on the facts and evidence in your case. For example, for less serious complaints, the Ministry might try mediation to get you and the business to reach an agreement that follows the rules. Or, if the Ministry thinks that the business broke the law, they might start a court case.
 If the Ministry is not able to help you, they will try to find an organization or government office that might be able to help. You can also think about suing the business in small claims court .
 Energy contracts
 Energy retailers are companies that offer contracts for electricity or natural gas. Door-to-door energy retailers must follow different rules.
 For information about these rules and what you can do if you have problems with energy retailers, read our questions on energy contracts.
