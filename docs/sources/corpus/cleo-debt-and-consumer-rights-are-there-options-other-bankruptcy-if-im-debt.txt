 Are there options other than bankruptcy if I&#8217;m in debt?

 Question and answer

 Are there options other than bankruptcy if I&#8217;m in debt?

 Next steps

 1. Get advice about your financial situation

 2. Talk to your creditors

 3. Make a debt repayment plan

 4. Apply for a consolidation loan

 5. File a consumer proposal

 Yes, there are other options that might work better for you than bankruptcy . For example, you can:

 get advice about your financial situation from a non-profit financial counsellor
 make a repayment plan with your creditors
 work with a financial advisor to plan how to pay off your debts or get a consolidation loan
 prepare and file a consumer proposal with a Licensed Insolvency Trustee
