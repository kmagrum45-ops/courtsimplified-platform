 Can I still owe money after I have been discharged from bankruptcy?

 Question and answer

 Can I still owe money after I have been discharged from bankruptcy?

 Next steps

 1. Find out which debts you still owe

 2. Talk with your Licensed Insolvency Trustee

 3. Make a repayment plan

 There are some debts that you still have to pay even if you file for bankruptcy . For example:

 secured debts you have for assets you plan to keep
 government student loans, if you have been out of school for less than 7 years
 child and spousal support payments
 fines and court penalties
 debts that are the result of a crime

 These debts survive your bankruptcy. Your Licensed Insolvency Trustee will tell you what debts you will still owe after bankruptcy.
 Usually, after your bankruptcy is discharged, you do not owe your creditors money anymore. But a creditor might still ask you to pay them back after the discharge . It's a good idea to ask your trustee if you are supposed to pay that creditor. If you do not have to pay, the trustee will tell the creditor to stop contacting you.
