 How do I change a claim I filed with the Small Claims online service?

 Next steps

 1. Figure out if you can amend your claim

 2. Amend your claim online

 3. Serve the defendant

 4. File your documents with the court

 You might be able to change a claim you made using the Small Claims Court e-filing service .
 Changing your claim is called amending. There is no fee to amend your claim. But you can only amend your claim online if you haven't served it on the person or business you're suing.
 If you've already served your claim on the defendant , you may be able to amend your claim in person at the courthouse using paper forms.
 Legal help
 If you earn a low income, you may be able to get legal help from Pro Bono Ontario . They offer services in some court locations in Toronto and Ottawa, and a legal advice hotline where you can get up to 30 minutes of free legal advice.
