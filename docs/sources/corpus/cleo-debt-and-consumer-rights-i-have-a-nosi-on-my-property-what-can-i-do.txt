 I have a NOSI on my property. What can I do?

 Next steps

 1. Learn about NOSIs

 2. Learn about the NOSI scam

 3. Cancel your contract

 4. Get legal help

 A notice of security interest (NOSI) is a legal document that a company puts on the title of someone's home. The NOSI lets people know there's a security fixture in the home that's being rented or financed. A security fixture is usually equipment like HVAC units, water heaters, and furnaces.
 When you finance an item, it means that a company lends you money so you can buy it. You usually agree to pay off the loan in monthly payments.
 Starting June 1, 2024 in Ontario, companies are not allowed to put a NOSI on the title of your home when you rent or finance an item from them. 
 If a company has already put a NOSI on your home, that NOSI is now expired. You do not need to pay anything to the company to remove the NOSI. You may need to have the NOSI removed if you want to sell your home or refinance your mortgage.  If you need to remove a NOSI from your home, get help from a real estate lawyer . 
 Even though the company can no longer use the NOSI to get back any money you owe them, you may still need to make your rental payments for the security fixture.

 Scam alert: If a company calls you offering to remove a NOSI from your home for a fee, that is a scam . NOSIs that companies have put on homes are now expired. This means that you usually will not need to remove a NOSI from your home unless you want to sell your home or refinance your mortgage.
 If you do want to remove a NOSI from your home you should get help from a real estate lawyer . Do not pay money to any company that offers to help you remove a NOSI from your home.
 If any company calls you about removing a NOSI, you can report them to your local police or call the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre at 1-888-495-8501.
