Hours
Please note that members of the public wishing to issue or file documentation related to Criminal, Small Claims Court, Enforcement, Civil, Family or Estate matters, should attend the court administrative offices located at 41 Dundas Street West, Napanee, ON.
To appear in court for Superior Court matters such as Family, Civil or Superior Court Criminal, members of the public should attend the Courthouse located at 97 Thomas Street, Napanee, ON.
To appear in court for Ontario Court matters and Small Claims Court matters, members of the public should attend the court located at 41 Dundas Street West, Napanee, ON.

Accessibility accommodations
Contact the accessibility coordinator as early as possible if you: need disability accommodations for a court proceeding
have any questions or concerns about accessibility
When requesting disability accommodation, provide the following information:
court address
court file number
date and time of the court proceedings
type of accommodation you need (for example, assistive listening devices, a temporary wheelchair, real-time captioning)
If you don’t have all the information listed above, you can still contact the accessibility coordinator for help.
Learn more about the accessibility resources available in Ontario courts .
