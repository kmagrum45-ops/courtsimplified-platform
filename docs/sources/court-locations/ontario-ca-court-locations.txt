Hours
At this time, counter services are offered from Monday to Friday, from 9:00 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. and 2:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m. (excluding holidays).
A satellite court provides selected and limited court services in conjunction with a main court office. Please contact the satellite court office for hours of operation.
Accessibility
Many of our courthouses have accessibility features for people with disabilities. If you have any questions about the courthouse’s accessibility features, or if you need accessible court services, please contact the Accessibility Coordinator listed under that location.
Have an upcoming court appearance?
You can access the daily court lists for more details on your upcoming court appearance.
Satellite or fly-in court locations If your event is scheduled at a satellite or fly-in court, it may not be listed under the daily court lists. For more information, you will need to:
check the main court location
contact the satellite court or base court
Online and weekend bail courts Online court lists for weekend bail courts are not available at this time.
Visit the  municipal Provincial Offences Court contact page  for more information about finding a municipal court location near you.
Online court filing
Where possible, consider online filing of your civil claims , small claims , or family law matters.
